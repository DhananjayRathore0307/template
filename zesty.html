Our Story & Ethos

Crafting Moments Around The Table

Founded on the premise that fine dining should be rich with soul and free of pretension, Zesty brings together classical European techniques with contemporary culinary innovation.

Every plate starts with a genuine relationship — with organic growers in the valley, day-boat fishermen, and small-batch biodynamic vineyards. We cook with absolute respect for what each season brings.
