OUR STORY

Started in a garage, kept the habit of caring too much

In 2016 we began roasting six-kilo batches in a rented garage because we couldn't find coffee in the city that tasted like the farms we'd visited. Amber & Oak grew from that stubbornness — every cup is still cupped and tasted before it goes on the menu.
