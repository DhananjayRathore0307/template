About Eclipse

Eclipse began as a single dim room and a short menu built entirely around what the market offered that morning. A decade later, the room has grown, the menu still changes daily, and the kitchen still answers to nothing but the season.

Head Chef

The Chef

Trained in Lyon and shaped by a decade of small kitchens, our chef treats every plate as a short, deliberate sentence — nothing added that doesn't need to be there.
