Our Story

Started with one cart
and a stubborn recipe

Plantify started as a single cart at a Sunday market, built on the idea that plant-based food shouldn't taste like a compromise. Four years later we're still cooking from the same three principles: whole ingredients, bold spice, and nothing pretending to be something it's not.
